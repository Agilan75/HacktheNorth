# M8 — /verdict and /verify-fix

Files: `apps/mobile/app/verdict.tsx`, `apps/mobile/app/verify-fix.tsx`. No other file touched.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why | Rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| M8-1 | **The client does no arithmetic on any number the API returns.** The breakdown shows each `price.factors[]` entry as the API returned it (`name`, `input`, `× factor`) and the API's `predictedMonthlyPremium` / `predictedPremium`. The only client-side work is formatting, and comparing a factor with 1 to pick the words "raises your price" / "lowers your price" / "no change". | PRD 1 invariant: no client decides a number. | Showing a computed base rate (`monthly ÷ Π factors`), which the DTO does not carry. |
| M8-2 | **The price drop on /verify-fix is shown as before and after, not as a computed "you save $X".** Before = the monthly estimate from the result the screen held, after = `response.result.price.predictedMonthlyPremium`. Annual figures come from `response.before/after.predictedPremium`. The screen says in words whether the new number is lower, higher or the same (a comparison), strikes through the old price when it drops, and adds an ↓/↑/= glyph, so colour never carries the meaning alone. | Same invariant, and PRD 6.7 wants a subtraction "the user can check": both numbers are on screen. | Printing a client-side difference. |
| M8-3 | **The fix card uses the API's flip**: each move's `fixHint ?? label`, `flip.verdictAfter`, and `premiumBefore` → `premiumAfter` (annual, because that is what `Flip` carries). If there's no flip, it shows `flip.reason`. If the result is FIT, it says nothing needs fixing. | These are the engine's numbers, and the tenant copy only relabels them. | Estimating a monthly saving on the phone. |
| M8-4 | **Which hazard to verify**: hazards are the `hazard.<key>` entries in `price.factors` (exactly what was priced in). By default the Verify button uses the first flip move that is a hazard (`hazardPortableHeater` → `portableHeater`). Otherwise it uses the first priced hazard. /verify-fix shows a radio group whenever more than one hazard is priced. `hazardKey` is sent as the bare key, which the API's `hazardKeyOf` accepts. | This needs no extra endpoint, and the user can still change which hazard they are verifying. | A separate hazard list from observations. Observations are not all priced. |
| M8-5 | **Route params**: `/verdict?id=<sweepId>`, `/verify-fix?id=<sweepId>&hazard=<key>`. Both are optional and fall back to `sessionStore.sweepId`. Hazard rows in the breakdown link to `/hazard/[id]` with **id = the hazard key** (e.g. `portableHeater`). | M7's stub pushes `/verdict` with no params, so the session fallback keeps that working. M9 owns `/hazard/[id]` and must read `id` as a hazard key. If M9 chose observation ids instead, the link lands on its "not found" state and nothing crashes. | Requiring params. |
| M8-6 | **/verdict re-fetches `GET /sweeps/:id` on every focus** (`useFocusEffect`). Only the first load shows skeletons. | Coming back from /verify-fix must show the new price and verdict without a manual refresh. | Passing the result back through router params. |
| M8-7 | **Photo path**: "Take a photo" (`launchCameraAsync`) and "Choose a photo from my library" (`launchImageLibraryAsync`) are two full-width buttons of equal size, one under the other. The photo is resized to 1280 px wide JPEG q0.7 with the object API of `expo-image-manipulator` (`manipulateAsync` is deprecated) and sent as bare base64. If camera permission is denied, the screen says so and points to the library button. | An inclusivity requirement (PRD 11): the library is a peer, not a fallback. The resize also keeps a 12 MP photo from blowing the 60 s upload deadline. | Camera only. |
| M8-8 | **Next-step sheet** is an RN `Modal` (`pageSheet`) showing one sentence per verdict and then actions: answer the remaining questions (only when `stage === 'questions'`), Verify my fix, Scan another room (resets the session and goes to /new), See all my rooms, Close. | The DTOs have no share-slug creation for sweeps, so there is no "share" step. | Linking to /s/[slug] without a slug. |
| M8-9 | **Tenant wording for verdicts**: FIT "Good to cover", REFER "A person needs to review this", DOES_NOT_FIT "Can’t be covered as it is". These go in `VerdictPill.text`. The pill keeps its glyph and shape (filled vs outlined), and each screen adds a full sentence. | The console's "Fits appetite" is underwriter jargon. PRD 11 asks for plain language. | Reusing `VERDICT_STYLES.label`. |
| M8-10 | **Helpers (money formatting, hazard names, key mapping) are private to each screen file**, and are duplicated across the two. | M8 owns only these two files (AGENTS 1, 7), so it can't add a shared `src/lib` module. There are no vitest tests for M8, because the only logic is in route files that import react-native. | Creating `src/lib/verdict.ts`, which M8 does not own. |
| M8-11 | **Motion**: the "Now" card fades and springs in when the result arrives. It appears instantly under Reduce Motion. A success haptic plays when the price drops, and a warning haptic when the hazard is still there. VoiceOver announces the outcome and both prices. | The price drop is the demo moment, and it must also land for people who can't see it. | — |

## Human checklist (iPhone, Expo Go)

Nothing here has been run on a device. You need the API running and a sweep with at least one priced hazard. The seeded sweep, or any room with a portable heater or extension cord in view, will do.

1. **Verdict loads.** Finish a sweep and its questions. On "Your quote" you should see the room name, a large pill with a glyph and words ("● Good to cover" filled red, "◐ A person needs to review this" red outline, or "○ Can’t be covered as it is" black), and one sentence saying what it means.
2. **Estimate matches the API.** Compare the big monthly figure and the "a year" figure with `result.price.predictedMonthlyPremium` / `predictedPremium` from `GET /sweeps/:id`. They should match to the cent. The word "estimate" should appear under the price.
3. **Breakdown is the API's.** You should see one row per `price.factors[]` entry in the same order: contents, building age, each hazard, smoke detector (if known), term. Each row shows the input text, "× factor", and "raises / lowers your price / no change". Rows for hazards have a "See what we found ›" link that opens /hazard/[key].
4. **Why + rule.** "Why" shows the first reason sentence. "Show the rule we used" expands the citation (doc · section and the quote). VoiceOver should say "expanded"/"collapsed".
5. **Fix.** With a flip, you should see the hint lines and "If you do this: …, Estimate $A a year, instead of $B a year".
6. **Verify, camera.** Tap "Verify my fix", then "Take a photo". A permission prompt appears the first time, then the camera. Take a photo of the spot with the hazard removed. You should see a preview, and "Check my fix" becomes enabled. Tap it. The button shows a spinner and "Checking your photo…" for up to about a minute.
7. **The drop.** With the hazard really gone, you should see a "Done: The … is gone." notice with the model's reason, then BEFORE and NOW cards. The BEFORE price is struck through, NOW springs in, you get a success haptic, and the sentence "↓ Your estimate went down, from $X to $Y a month." Check that $Y matches the new `GET /sweeps/:id`.
8. **Still there.** Photograph the hazard itself. You should see "We can still see the …", the price unchanged ("= Your estimate stayed at …"), and a warning haptic.
9. **Back to quote.** Tap "Back to my quote". The verdict screen should show the new price and no longer list that hazard's factor, with no manual refresh.
10. **Library path.** Deny camera permission in Settings, then retry. You should see a plain message pointing at "Choose a photo from my library", and the library picker should work end to end.
11. **Several hazards.** With 2 or more priced hazards, /verify-fix shows "What did you fix?" radios. Choosing another one clears the old photo.
12. **Next-step sheet.** "What happens next" slides up a sheet. Every button works, "Close" dismisses it, and VoiceOver focus stays inside the sheet.
13. **Accessibility.** At AX5 text size, nothing clips and the cards wrap to one column. With VoiceOver, every button has a name and a hint, every factor row reads as one sentence, and the BEFORE/NOW cards each read as one sentence. In Grayscale (Color Filters), FIT, REFER and DOES_NOT_FIT stay distinguishable by glyph and words, and the price drop by strikethrough and "↓".
14. **Reduce Motion on.** The NOW card appears without the spring.
15. **Errors.** Stop the API and open /verdict. After about 5 s of retries you should see a "Problem: …" notice with "Try again" and no crash.
