# V04 — invariants: core + monotonic

## D1. Re-evaluation goes through an injected probe

**Decision.** `core.ts` exports `setInvariantProbe(probe | null)`,
`getInvariantProbe()`, and the types `InvariantProbe = (input: NaiveInput) => ProbedResult`
and `ProbedResult = EngineResultView & { predictedPremium?, confidence? }`.
Determinism and both monotonicity invariants re-run the engine through the probe.

**Why.** The frozen `Invariant` signature is `(testCase, result)`: one case, one
result. Determinism and monotonicity are relations between two evaluations, and
`packages/verify` may not import `@retrofit/engine` outside compare.ts/worker.ts.
The probe lets V08's worker (which owns the engine adapter) hand one in, with no
frozen signature changed. The additions are new exports in V04's own file; the
barrel's `export *` already surfaces them.

**Rejected.** A module-level memo alone (catches repeats only), or re-deriving
tiers from the guideline table inside the invariant (a third copy of the table,
which is layer B's job).

**V08 must:** call `setInvariantProbe(input => adapt(engine(input)))` once per
worker thread, and include `predictedPremium` (and `confidence` if available)
in what the probe returns so the premium invariant has something to check.

## D2. Fallbacks without a probe

- `determinism`: a bounded (20 000 entries) input→result memo; a repeated input
  with a different result is a violation.
- `scoreMonotonicInEveryFactor`: checks `score = 100 × Σ w·t` over the eight
  factors with the W-1 weights (T-SPAN). Non-negative weights make the score
  monotone in every tier, so this is the single-result half of the law.
- `premiumMonotonicInEveryFactor`: nothing to compare; reports nothing.

## D3. What "improving a factor" means

Submission type → `new_business`; line → `commercial_property`; state → `OH`;
TIV and premium move toward the middle of their unimodal target bands (75M,
87 500), halfway and all the way; pre-1990 share and five-year loss move down
(only when > 0); post-2010 share moves up to `1 − pre`; acceptable construction
moves toward 1; a missing factor filled with its best value; an open HIGH
contradiction resolved. After each move the score must not fall (1e-6) and no
factor's tier may fall.

## D4. Premium directions

Never cheaper when TIV, five-year loss, or pre-1990 share rises, or acceptable
construction share falls; never dearer for the opposite moves (PRD 6.7 "a worse
class is never cheaper"). Age moves shift share between the pre-1990 and
post-2010 buckets in the same direction. Tolerance `max(1e-6, 1e-9·|base|)`.
State, line and submission type are not rating inputs and are not moved.

## D5. Core checks beyond the PRD one-liners

Knockout set equals the factors with tier 0 (G-3); DOES_NOT_FIT only with a
knockout (V-1); with a knockout the deciding factor is a knockout factor (V-8a);
tier values ∈ {0, 0.6, 1, null}; completeness is a whole k/9 (V-6); FIT needs
100% completeness and incomplete knockout-free is REFER (V-2); confidence, when
reported, in [0, 1] (V-7). No invariant throws on NaN/null/garbage results.
