# M3 decisions — phone UI kit + ScanRing

Files: `apps/mobile/src/ui/kit.tsx`, `ScanRing.tsx`, `ringModel.ts` (+ `ringModel.test.ts`), `index.ts` (barrel; import as `@/ui` or `@/ui/kit`).

Kit exports: `Text`, `Heading`, `Button`, `Card`, `VerdictPill`, `Skeleton`, `SkeletonCard`, `Notice`, `TextField`, `ChoiceGroup`, `Screen`, `typeStyle`, `useFontScale`, and re-exported `COLORS`/`SPACE`/`RADIUS`/`MIN_TOUCH_TARGET`. Ring exports: `ScanRing`, plus the pure model (`ringSummary`, `markCovered`, `segmentForHeading`, `coveredPercent`, `bearingWords`, ...).

## Decisions

1. **M3-1 Dynamic type = RN's own font scaling, not `textStyle(token, fontScale)`.** RN already multiplies `fontSize`/`lineHeight` by the OS scale when `allowFontScaling` is on; feeding D01's scaled values in too would double-scale. The kit passes unscaled §13 steps and never fixes a height on anything with text. Only `display`/`title` are capped at 2x (a heading at AX5 would push the screen off); body text is uncapped. D01's scaled helper is used only to size Skeleton lines so placeholders grow with text. Rejected: `maxFontSizeMultiplier` on all text.
2. **M3-2 No Fraunces/Inter on the phone.** No font assets are bundled and `_layout.tsx` (frozen) loads none; an unregistered `fontFamily` errors on iOS. Display steps use Georgia (iOS) / serif (Android), body uses the system font. Rejected: setting `fontFamily: 'Fraunces'` and hoping.
3. **M3-3 `muted` tone renders Muted deep** (D01-1: Muted fails AA at body sizes).
4. **M3-4 VerdictPill always renders a glyph (●/◐/○ from `VERDICT_MARKS`) plus words** (default `VERDICT_STYLES.label`, `wording="short"` for FIT/REFER/DOES NOT FIT, or `text` for tenant copy). Border drawn at 2pt so REFER's outline reads as a shape. Screen-reader label: "Result: <words>". Pills are labels, not buttons (D01-6).
5. **M3-5 ScanRing is display-only.** It takes the 36-panel mask and heading from the caller (M2's machine during capture, the API's `CoverageResult.panels`/`coveragePct` after). It computes a progress readout for the person holding the phone, never a verdict/price/hazard; when the API's `coveragePct` is passed it wins. Percent is floored so 74.9 never reads as 75.
6. **M3-6 Covered vs not is fill vs outline**, not only red vs grey. Heading is a triangle outside the ring. Bearing 0 at the top, clockwise, same convention as `engine/sweep/geometry`.
7. **M3-7 Turn hint = shortest turn to the nearest unscanned segment centre**, rounded to 10° (min 10°), ties clockwise. "Hold steady" when already pointing at a gap. `gapName` lets a caller say "the window side"; the ring itself cannot know what is in a gap. Rejected: pointing at the largest gap (makes people swing past small holes next to them).
8. **M3-8 Screen reader:** the whole ring is one `progressbar` element; `accessibilityValue.text` is the sentence ("61 percent of the room scanned. Turn left about 30 degrees to cover the part you have not scanned yet."), plus any radar markers in words. `announce` speaks only at 25% steps and when Finish unlocks — not per heading tick. The same hint is printed under the ring for sighted users.
9. **M3-9 Pure model in `src/ui/ringModel.ts`, not `src/lib/`.** M3 owns `src/ui/**`; `src/lib/` belongs to other units. The vitest `mobile` project includes `src/**/*.test.ts`, so it still runs in node.
10. **M3-10 Skeleton uses RN `Animated`, not Reanimated**, and stops pulsing when Reduce Motion is on. Avoids depending on the worklets babel setup for a loader.
11. **M3-11 Screen** uses `SafeAreaView` from `react-native-safe-area-context` with edges bottom/left/right (the stack header owns the top); expo-router provides the provider.

## Not mine, noticed

## Human checklist (iPhone, Expo Go)

Nothing below has been run on a device. Put `<ScanRing panels={...} headingDeg={...} />` and a few kit components on any screen.

1. **Bundles:** app opens without a red "Unable to resolve" screen (see the `.js` note above).
2. **Ring renders:** 36 separate tiles in a circle; scanned ones solid red, unscanned ones pale with a grey outline; a black triangle outside the ring. Works: you can count gaps between tiles and tell scanned from unscanned in Settings > Accessibility > Display > Color Filters > Grayscale.
3. **Heading marker follows the phone:** turn on the spot during a sweep; the triangle moves clockwise when you turn right and tiles fill under it. Works: a full slow turn fills the ring and the middle reads 100%.
4. **Hint direction is right:** with half the ring filled, point at a filled part; the text says "Turn left/right about N degrees"; turning that way reaches pale tiles in roughly N degrees.
5. **VoiceOver:** focus the ring; it reads "Room scan coverage, progress bar/…, 61 percent of the room scanned. Turn …". With `announce`, it speaks at 25/50/75/100% and "enough to finish", and is silent between.
6. **Dynamic type:** Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text, max (AX5). Buttons grow taller instead of clipping; body text is huge; titles grow but stop at 2x; the ring percent stays inside the ring.
7. **Touch targets:** every Button, ChoiceGroup option and pressable Card is at least a fingertip (44pt); VoiceOver announces each with a role ("button", "radio button, selected").
8. **VerdictPill:** FIT = red filled with "● Fits appetite", REFER = red outline with "◐ Refer to underwriter", DOES_NOT_FIT = black filled with "○ Outside appetite"; VoiceOver reads "Result: …".
9. **Reduce Motion on:** skeletons are still grey bars, not pulsing.
10. **Safe area:** `Screen` footer buttons sit above the home indicator; nothing hides under the notch in landscape.
11. **Skia in Expo Go:** if the ring area is blank or crashes, Skia 2.12 is not in this Expo Go build; the text hint under the ring still carries all the information.
