# M2 — heading filter, capture loop, session store

Files: `apps/mobile/src/lib/heading.ts`, `capture.ts`, `session.ts` + `*.test.ts` (55 tests, node).

## Decisions

1. **Paths follow the Run 3 task prompt (`src/lib/*`), not OWNERSHIP.json (`src/sweep/heading.ts`, `src/sweep/machine.ts`, `src/store/session.ts`).** The prompt wins on what to build (AGENTS.md), R3-1 says the mobile vitest project covers `src/lib`, and sibling units were told `src/lib`. Nothing was created at the OWNERSHIP.json paths.
2. **Magnetic heading, never mixed with true heading.** `headingFromReading` takes `magHeading`, then `trueHeading`, else null. Bearings are relative to the start, so declination does not matter, and `trueHeading` is -1 without location permission. Switching sources mid-sweep would jump by the declination.
3. **The filter works on the wrapped delta**: `next = normalize(prev + alpha * signedDelta(prev, raw))`, alpha 0.25, and the first reading seeds it. So 358 -> 2 moves through 0 and never swings through 180.
4. **Bearings are relative to the first heading sample** (0 = sweep start), matching `SweepFrameDto.bearingDeg`. The 36 panels are relative too, so the ring's panel 0 is wherever the user started.
5. **Coverage fills from the heading, not only from frames.** Every panel the filtered heading passes through (the short way) fills. Frames are capped at 15 with steps of at least 10°, so 15 frames alone could never reach 75%. The ring keeps filling after the cap. A jump of more than 60° between two samples counts as a compass glitch: only the panel it lands in fills.
6. **Capture gate**: the machine asks for a frame (`pending`) when all of these hold: it is sweeping, nothing is pending, fewer than 15 frames, at least 1200 ms since the last *request*, and at least 10° (shortest arc) from the last *captured* bearing. The first sample captures straight away. The screen takes the picture and dispatches `captured` or `captureFailed`. A failure clears `pending`, and the next heading sample tries again. `captureGate()` reports why no frame was taken.
7. **Finish needs 27 of 36 panels (compared as integers, so exactly 75% passes) and at least 1 frame** (the API rejects 0). `coveragePctDisplay` rounds down, so the screen never shows "75%" while Finish is still locked.
8. **Turn advice points to the nearer edge of the largest gap.** Ties between equal gaps go to the lowest panel. `summarize().hint` is a plain-language line with no colour words, for the screen and VoiceOver.
9. **Upload path**: `uploadBearings(n)` spaces picked photos evenly (3 -> 0/120/240, PRD 11), capped at 15. The store records `source: 'upload' | 'sweep'`. Starting a sweep after an upload clears the uploaded frames first.
10. **The session store also holds `sweepId`** (set after POST /sweeps), which /analyzing and later screens need. It is a small hand-written external store with `useSession(selector?)` built on React's `useSyncExternalStore`. It imports `react`, never `react-native`. `buildCreateRequest` builds the `SweepCreateRequestDto` body, or returns plain-language problems (`SESSION_PROBLEM_TEXT`). It trims the room label, strips `data:` prefixes, and clamps pitch to ±90.
11. The client-side coverage only gates Finish and drives the ring. The API's `CoverageResult` is the number that counts.

## Human checklist (iPhone, Expo Go)

These can only be checked on a device, and they need M6's /sweep screen wired to these modules.

1. **Wrap-around on a real compass.** Stand facing roughly north (Compass app says 350–10°) and start a sweep. Turn slowly right through north. Pass: the coverage % only goes up, the ring fills next to where you started, and there is no jump to the opposite side of the ring.
2. **Noise at rest.** Hold the phone still for 10 s after the first frame. Pass: the frame count stays at 1. If it climbs, the compass is jittering by 10° or more, so lower `DEFAULT_HEADING_ALPHA` (for example to 0.15).
3. **Cadence.** Turn at a steady walking pace. Pass: you feel one haptic about every 1–2 s, never two within about 1 s, and it stops at 15 frames while the ring keeps filling.
4. **Finish threshold.** Turn about 250° and stop. Pass: Finish is disabled and the hint says to turn. Turn about 20° more. Pass: Finish enables as the display reaches 75%, and never earlier.
5. **Turn hint direction.** After turning about 270° to the right, the hint should say "Turn right about N degrees". Face back toward the start, and it should switch to "Turn left" whenever the gap is closer that way. Check that left and right match the physical direction (right = clockwise, seen from above).
6. **Magnetic interference.** Sweep next to a fridge or a steel beam. Pass: glitches either fill a single panel or none. The ring never fills a big arc you did not turn through.
7. **VoiceOver.** With VoiceOver on, the hint text is read out and has no colour words.
8. **Upload path.** Pick 3 photos. Pass: the request carries bearings 0, 120 and 240, and the room goes through analysis.
