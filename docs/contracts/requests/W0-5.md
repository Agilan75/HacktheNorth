## BLOCKER 1 — `completeness` denominator: 9 (engine) vs 8 (naive)

`INTERPRETATIONS.md` **V-6**: completeness is over the **nine** required
components 0–8 of `vectors/commercial.json` (B11 asserts `8/9 = 88.888…%`).
`NAIVE_SPEC.md` **§5.3**: `completeness = 100 × (known appetite factors ÷ 8)`.

`building_age` spans **two** components (5 `pctTivPre1990`, 6 `pctTivPost2010`),
so the two denominators can never agree. `EngineResultView.completeness` is a
compared field (`packages/verify/src/types.ts:102`), so **every generated case
with any missing component is a disagreement.** At the planned masking rates
that is millions of false failures.

**Fix (pick one, state it in both files):** amend `NAIVE_SPEC.md` §5.3 to
`completeness = 100 × (known required components ÷ 9)`, counting
`pctTivPre1990` and `pctTivPost2010` as two separate components that are always
jointly known or jointly missing. Add the same sentence to INTERPRETATIONS V-6.

## BLOCKER 2 — the pre-1990 REFER path differs between the two oracles

`INTERPRETATIONS.md` **R-AGE-REFER**: REFER iff `0 < pctTivPre1990 ≤ 0.5`;
the row `pctTivPre1990 == 0` says **"No refer flag."**

`NAIVE_SPEC.md` **§5.5 step 4**: REFER when `pctTivPre1990 > 0 && ≤ 0.5`
**or** `anyBuildingPre1990 === true` while `pctTivPre1990 ≤ 0.5`.

`NaiveInput.anyBuildingPre1990` is an independent generated field, and the case
is physically reachable (a pre-1990 building whose TIV is unknown is excluded
from the share but not from "any building"). Engine returns `FIT`, naive returns
`REFER`. `verdict` is compared. False failures on every such case.

**Fix:** delete the `anyBuildingPre1990` clause from `NAIVE_SPEC.md` §5.5 step 4
so both sides key only on `0 < pctTivPre1990 ≤ 0.5`; or add a row to
INTERPRETATIONS R-AGE-REFER making `anyBuildingPre1990 === true` with
`pctTivPre1990 == 0` a REFER, and have E03 populate `rollup.pre1990BuildingIds`
from unknown-TIV buildings too. One of the two, written into both files.

## BLOCKER 3 — "lowest-scoring" factor is undefined, and it is a compared field

`INTERPRETATIONS.md` **V-8**: "else the lowest-scoring fired appetite rule
(ties broken by factor order)". `NAIVE_SPEC.md` §5.6: "the lowest-scoring known
factor". Neither says whether "score" means **points** (`100·w·t`) or **tier
value** (`t`).

They differ: `building_age` at `t=0.6, w=0.10` → 6 points; `tiv` at
`t=0.6, w=0.15` → 9 points. By points the deciding factor is `building_age`;
by tier value it is a tie broken to `tiv` (index 3 < 5). `decidingFactorId` is
in `EngineResultView` and is compared exactly.

**Fix:** in INTERPRETATIONS V-8 and NAIVE_SPEC §5.6, write: "lowest `tierValue`
among known factors; ties broken by the §2 factor order" (or "lowest `points`"),
and say which. Also state the deciding factor when **every** factor is missing
(`null`) and when the REFER is `missing_data` (no refer factor → fall through).

## BLOCKER 5 — the engine data directories have two addresses

`OWNERSHIP.json` and `UNITS.json` put the rulebooks, rating tables and questions
under `packages/engine/src/{rules,rating,questions}/`. `docs/contracts/requests/W0-2.md`
§2 and `NAIVE_SPEC.md` §2 (the forbidden list) both say they sit **beside**
`src/`, like `vectors/`, and `data.ts:dataFilePath()` resolves from
`import.meta.url`. Four units (E13, E14, E15, E17) plus `data.ts` (E13) and
`GET /rules` (A11) must all pick the same one; if E13 writes
`packages/engine/rules/commercial.json` and E17 writes
`packages/engine/src/rating/commercial.json`, `readRulebook`/`readRatingTable`
cannot both work, and `rootDir: "src"` would put the JSON in the emit path.

**Fix:** settle on `packages/engine/{rules,rating,questions}/` (beside
`vectors/`, matching W0-2's note) and rewrite those 10 paths in `OWNERSHIP.json`
and `UNITS.json`.

## MAJOR 6 — `lossRatio` in the quality index has no definition anywhere

PRD §6.8 and INTERPRETATIONS **P-5** both use `0.15 × (1 − lossRatio)′` and
`QualityComponents.lossRatio` exists in `types.ts`, but no file defines
`lossRatio`. Candidates: `expectedAnnualLoss ÷ quotedPremium`,
`÷ predictedPremium`, or `fiveYearLoss ÷ 5 ÷ premium`. E10 will guess; C04/C14
and I1 assert on rank order. Also undefined: how `adequacy′` (a ratio near 1) is
"scaled to 0–100" — `clamp01(adequacy)·100`, or `min(adequacy, 2)/2·100`?

**Fix:** add two rows to INTERPRETATIONS §5 "Peers and rank" giving the exact
expressions, plus the value used when premium or expected loss is null.

## MAJOR 7 — what goes in `t[5]` and `t[6]` is unspecified

One factor (`building_age`) drives two vector components. `FeatureVector.t` is
per-component (11 entries) but the score is defined per-factor
(INTERPRETATIONS §1: "over the eight appetite factors"). Nothing says whether
both components carry the factor's tier value (a naive `w · t` over components
then double-counts `building_age` and under-counts nothing else) or only one
does. E05 `tiersFor`, E06 `evaluate`, the console Vector panel (C10) and V05's
vector invariants each need the same answer.

**Fix:** one line in INTERPRETATIONS §1: "a factor spanning several components
writes its tier value into every one of them; the score sums over the eight
factors, never over components."
